Section 04

Bottom line

  • 2 fully confirmed genuine defects from this run — a dead "PVR Cinemas" footer link (LGL-036) and a non-functional News category filter (NWS-019/020) — plus 33 unconfirmed candidates worth a manual pass.
  • 82 raw failures ≠ 82 bugs. 47 of them are sandbox network drops, resource-timeout noise, and one systemic Movie Details data-volatility issue — not product defects.
  • 219 skips are overwhelmingly scope blockers — Admin Panel access, backend contracts, hardware/OAuth — not gaps in test coverage.
